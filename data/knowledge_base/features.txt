StockPilot Features

INVENTORY CONTROL
- Real-time stock levels across every location, updated as sales and deliveries are recorded.
- Low-stock and out-of-stock alerts by email, push notification or in-app banner. Reorder points can be set per product and per location.
- Purchase orders: create, send to suppliers, and receive stock against a PO. Partial receipts are supported.
- Stock adjustments with a required reason code and full audit trail.
- Batch and expiry date tracking for perishable goods.
- Barcode and QR scanning from the phone camera, or from a Bluetooth scanner. Bulk label printing supports common label sizes.
- Multi-location transfers: request stock from another store, track it in transit, and receive it at the destination.

SALES AND REPORTING
- Sales dashboard with revenue, units sold, gross margin and stock turn by location.
- Best sellers and slow movers report, useful for planning purchase orders.
- Inventory valuation using FIFO or weighted average cost.
- Export any report to CSV or schedule it as a daily email.
- Simple demand forecast for the next 30 days based on the last 12 weeks of sales. The forecast is a guide, not a guarantee.

OPERATIONS
- Offline mode on the mobile app: sales and stock adjustments queue locally and sync automatically when the connection returns. Conflicts are flagged for review.
- Role-based permissions: owner, manager, stock clerk and read-only auditor.
- Multi-currency and multi-warehouse support.
- Supplier and customer records with contact history.
- CSV import for products and opening stock, with a validation preview before import.
- REST API for custom integrations, with API keys managed per location.

MOBILE AND DEVICES
- iOS and Android apps included in every plan.
- Works on inexpensive Android handhelds and on any phone with a camera.
- No proprietary hardware required.

RELIABILITY AND SECURITY
- Data encrypted at rest (AES-256) and in transit (TLS 1.2+).
- Daily automated backups with 30-day retention on Growth and Enterprise.
- SOC 2 Type II certified; security documentation available under NDA.
- 99.9% uptime target; public status page lists incidents and maintenance windows.
- Data export at any time; customers own their data and can request deletion.

LIMITATIONS TO BE HONEST ABOUT
- Manufacturing bills of materials and production planning are not supported.
- Native accounting is not included; StockPilot syncs to accounting software instead.
- Forecasts are simple statistical estimates and should not replace merchandising judgment.
