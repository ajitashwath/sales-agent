StockPilot Pricing (2026)

StockPilot is cloud-based inventory software for small retailers. Pricing is per store location, billed monthly or annually. All plans include a 14-day free trial with no credit card required, and there is no setup fee.

PLANS

Starter - $49 per store per month
- 1 store location included
- Up to 3 user seats
- Real-time stock levels and low-stock alerts
- Barcode scanning from the mobile app
- Purchase orders and supplier records
- Standard email support (next business day)

Growth - $99 per store per month
- Everything in Starter
- Up to 10 user seats per location
- Multi-location transfers and consolidated reporting
- Sales and margin analytics dashboard
- POS integrations (Shopify, WooCommerce, Square)
- Priority support with 4-hour first response during business hours
- Optional onboarding package: $299 one-time (two guided sessions plus data migration review)

Enterprise - custom pricing
- Unlimited store locations and user seats
- Role-based access control and audit log export
- Dedicated account manager and 99.9% uptime SLA
- API access with higher rate limits
- SSO via Google Workspace or Microsoft Entra ID

DISCOUNTS AND BILLING
- Annual billing saves 20% compared to monthly.
- Non-profits and registered educational institutions receive 15% off any plan.
- Retailers migrating from a competitor can request a one-time migration credit of up to $500, approved case by case.
- Prices are in USD and exclude applicable taxes (GST/VAT charged where required).
- We accept major credit cards, UPI and bank transfer for annual plans.
- Invoices can be issued to a company name and GST number on request.

WHAT IS NOT INCLUDED
- Hardware such as barcode scanners and label printers is not included; we recommend Bluetooth scanners that work with our mobile app.
- Custom development work is quoted separately and typically starts at $2,000.
- Additional user seats beyond the plan limit cost $12 per seat per month.

TRIAL AND CANCELLATION
- Cancel anytime; access continues until the end of the paid period.
- We offer a 30-day money-back guarantee on the first paid invoice.
- Downgrading from Growth to Starter keeps data for 90 days in read-only mode.
